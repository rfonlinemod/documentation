Level 40 – 45
Quest Guide

Take on the hunt. Conquer the mission. Claim your reward — a new run begins every 20 minutes!

📍 Locate the Quest NPCs

  • Elan (Quests 6–10): the quest NPC awaits you near town and the PvP area.
  • Volcanic Cauldron (Quests 1–5): the quest NPC stands guard at the VC Entrance.

🎯 How to Find Your Targets

  • Green circle on the map: every target’s hunting ground is ringed by a green circle on your radar (top-right) and on the full-screen map (press M). Head to the circle to find the monsters.
  • Hover to read the name: on the full-screen map, hover your mouse over a green circle to pop up the exact monster name(s) to hunt at that spot.
  • Quest mark overhead: each target monster carries a quest marker floating above its head, so you can pick out the right mob at a glance.
  • Always visible: the circles and marks show even before you accept the quest — scout the spot first, then take the job when you’re ready.

The full-screen map (M) ringed with green quest circles — hover one to reveal the monster name.
